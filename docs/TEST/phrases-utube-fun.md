## 成语新说 (old saying, new telling)

| idiom_zh | pinyin | original_zh | original_en | joke_zh | joke_en | pun_fun_note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **寡言少语** | guǎ yán shǎo yǔ | 形容说话极少。 | Taciturn; a person of very few words. | 寡妇因为心里郁闷很少说话 | A widow rarely speaks because she is feeling depressed. | Reinterprets **寡** (few/scarce) as **寡妇** (widow). |
| **四十不惑** | sì shí bù huò | 人到了四十岁，具备判断力，不再感到困惑（语出《论语》）。 | At forty, one reaches a mature age of clarity and no longer suffers from doubt. | 快40了，还是经不住诱惑 | Nearly 40 years old, and still cannot resist temptation. | Reinterprets **惑** (doubt) as **诱惑** (temptation). |
| **知足常乐** | zhī zú cháng lè | 知道满足就能经常保持快乐。 | Contentment brings happiness (knowing when enough is enough). | 你知道有人请你洗脚，就会经常感到快乐 | Knowing someone is treating you to a foot massage will make you constantly happy. | Reinterprets **足** (enough/contentment) literally as **脚/足** (feet). |
| **一穷二白** | yī qióng èr bái | 形容基础薄弱，既贫穷又文化落后（像一张白纸）。 | Impoverished and blank (poor with a blank canvas). | 人一旦变穷，别人就会用两个白眼看着你 | Once a person becomes poor, others look at them with two cold side-eyes. | Reinterprets **二白** (secondly blank) literally as **两个白眼** (two side-eyes / cold stares). |
| **知书达理** | zhī shū dá lǐ | 指有文化、懂礼貌、通情达理。 | Well-educated, cultured, and reasonable. | 光知道书本上的知识不够用，还得学会送礼 | Just knowing book smarts isn't enough; you also have to learn how to give gifts (bribes). | Reinterprets **达** (understand) as **送达** (deliver) and **理** (logic) as **礼/礼物** (gifts). |
